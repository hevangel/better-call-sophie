# AI creative policy and rights

## Production philosophy

The show is intended to be **100% AI-generated as original production content**. The human showrunner and ChatGPT collaborate on direction, conception, decisions, prompt construction, review, and selection. The final AI-generated story, voices, images, and videos should be explicitly documented as such; "AI-generated" is not the same as "created without human direction."

The external repositories are *tools and references*, not AI-generated show content. Do not present open-source source code as proprietary original work.

## Creative integrity

- Disclose AI-assisted generation honestly in project materials and releases.
- Keep an audit trail for major images, scenes, voices, songs, and video clips, including review history and known tool/terms.
- Never represent ungenerated shots as finished; treat temporary art as concept art.
- Avoid unauthorized cloning of real people's likenesses or voices and do not present invented actors as real.
- Keep philosophical arguments intelligible and serious, even when characters argue strategically.

## Existing franchise and characters

The show references **Saul Goodman** and the franchise universe. These are third-party creative properties. The repository is an **unofficial speculative fan-fiction project**, not authorized or endorsed. Similar title/premise, characters, logos, footage, music, and images may implicate distinct rights. Copyright and trademark analysis must occur before public or commercial release. AI-made content does not by itself resolve franchise rights.

If later pursued commercially, consider securing rights or revising to a fully original family / title / setting.

## Tools and licensing

Review the terms of **Google Flow**, any model used, voice/music/audio providers, and all third-party submodules *at the time of use*. A tool being free/open-source does not automatically mean a model, training data, music, or output is cleared for all distribution. Submodules retain their own license terms; see `spec/third-party.md`.

## Data hygiene

Store no API keys, passwords, identifiable real client information, or full private account exports in Git. Back up expensive generated masters outside ordinary Git. Redact account identifiers in screenshots and provenance manifests.
