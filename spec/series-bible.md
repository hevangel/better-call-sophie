# Series bible — working draft (PROPOSED except where canon says LOCKED)

## Pitch

**Better Call Sophie** is an AI-generated philosophical drama/dark comedy about Dr. Sophie Goodman, Saul Goodman's granddaughter. Her grandfather defended the legally accused; she defends the morally accused. Her practice helps clients articulate, strengthen and publicly advocate positions that an audience, institution or opponent has condemned.

**Logline:** In a future where reputations can be destroyed by moral allegations faster than courts can intervene, a brilliant and commercially inventive philosopher builds a business defending the indefensible — only to discover that winning an argument and doing right are different things.

## Genre engine

Case-of-the-week disputes dramatize serious philosophical questions with real economic, personal or institutional stakes. Ongoing arcs follow Sophie's reputation, profit motive, family legacy, academic enemies and her ability to rationalize her own conduct.

A typical case has:
1. **Allegation**: someone is being judged, excluded, fired or publicly condemned.
2. **Client mandate**: what outcome matters, and which facts are genuinely known?
3. **Theory conflict**: at least two plausible philosophical approaches.
4. **Reframing**: Sophie challenges the facts, applied norm, normative theory or metaethical assumptions.
5. **Consequence**: victory at a board hearing, media exchange, institution, public debate or private relationship — not a fictional "moral court."
6. **Cost**: an ethical or emotional bill that arrives after the rhetorical triumph.

## Themes

- Advocacy versus truth; argument versus character.
- Moral pluralism and the commercial market for reasons.
- Technology and the democratization of expertise.
- Professional responsibility when expertise is sold to powerful clients.
- The Goodman family inheritance: ingenuity, performance, guilt, reinvention.

## Proposed setting

**Albuquerque, New Mexico, 2030** (era LOCKED; Albuquerque still PROPOSED). It is the near future, only four years ahead of today. A recognizable city rather than full cyberpunk spectacle. Sophie's inexpensive yet theatrically branded office holds philosophy books, a diagram-covered board, AI research systems, gaudy yellow-red advertising, and a burgundy-suited advocate. Corporate and institutional cases bring in other cities via travel and remote proceedings.

Google Flow is a **production tool** used to create this imagined world, not a feature of the story that must be mentioned in dialogue. In-world AI may be commonplace, but technology does not replace interesting human conflicts.

## Cast (all details outside canon pending)

- **Dr. Sophie Goodman** — sharp, charming PhD philosopher; enjoys adversarial argument; risks confusing clever defense with virtue. She starts **mixed** (LOCKED): principled about everyone's right to a defense, mercenary about fees and fame.
- **Rebecca Hart** — estranged mother, social worker; sees moral costs that Sophie discounts.
- **Professor Elena Salazar** — credible academic rival who believes philosophers owe more than strong arguments.
- **Daniel Park** — AI engineer/marketer who turns ideas into irresistible public communication.
- **Maria Ortega** — real lawyer and sometime ally; law remains a distinct institution.
- **Jimmy "Saul Goodman" McGill** — the legacy that Sophie admires and misunderstands; use only if rights/continuity direction is approved.
- **The Integrity Index** — possible private ethics-rating institution, subject to its own values and conflicts.

## Storytelling standards

Make both sides intellectually capable. Arguments must matter because characters risk something. Don't present moral error theory as a license for everything, or rhetorical success as proof of truth. Allow comedy through ads, colorful clients and Sophie's performance; let consequences land without jokes.

## Visual and sonic identity

Contrast gaudy Goodman yellow/red marketing with elegant, moody philosophical interiors; Albuquerque dusk and modern institutional spaces. Sophie concept anchor: dark wavy hair, cream blouse, burgundy suit, confident expression. Recurring characters, voices and locations need controlled reference packs before production; one-shot cameos and throwaway locations do not. See [style-guide.md](style-guide.md).
