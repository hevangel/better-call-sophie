# Supporting cast — PROPOSED

These are **not locked canon**. Change only with continuity review; see `spec/canon.md`.

## Rebecca Hart (working name)

Sophie's mother; a social worker who associates the Goodman legacy with damaged lives, not philosophical courage. Proposal: she was Saul's previously unknown daughter, and Sophie uses the Goodman name professionally against Rebecca's wishes. Timeline and plausibility need review.

## Professor Elena Salazar

Academic moral philosopher; former mentor and credible rival. She sees Sophie's work as the erosion of philosophy's duty to truth, yet cannot casually dismiss Sophie's strongest arguments.

## Daniel Park

AI engineer, researcher, and advertising strategist. Handles tooling and turns dense philosophical arguments into viral advocacy. A plausible source of opportunity and temptation.

## Maria Ortega

Practicing lawyer and professional collaborator; reminds Sophie that ethics consulting is not legal representation. A good source of courtroom/institutional realism.

## Jimmy McGill / Saul Goodman

Her grandfather. In the original show's ending, Jimmy serves an extended prison sentence and reckons with his Saul identity. Do not assume further appearances, chronology or actor-likeness rights are cleared. His arc should contradict Sophie's romanticized account of the family business.

## The Integrity Index

Potential private ethics-rating institution that sets practical standards for corporations and institutions. Philosophical assumptions embedded in ratings create an adversary with real stakes. Avoid portraying the Index as a magical legal authority.
