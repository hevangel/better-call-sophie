## Season through-line

Sophie monetizes the right to a strong defense. Each victory increases her notoriety and purchasing power while testing whether she can distinguish defending unpopular people from justifying the consequences of their actions. Proposed counterweight: an ethics-rating body and a respected philosopher whose theories have tangible effects.

## Pilot promise

The pilot must reveal Sophie's expertise through a consequential decision and a compelling human exchange; no prolonged exposition. Her business card / advertisement can supply short comic punctuation. The era is LOCKED as 2030; Albuquerque is still provisional. Before the pilot, a technical demo comes first (`production/demo/README.md`).
