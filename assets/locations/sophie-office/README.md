# Location: Goodman Philosophical Consulting — Sophie's office

**Status:** concept APPROVED (2026-11-22). **Floor plan v2 is APPROVED** (2026-11-22, with its four masters) after the showrunner's review of the first reference pack: the exterior and the interiors disagreed (corner unit vs. mid-row unit, door position), and the office needs privacy, with the waiting area and Sophie's own office as separate rooms. Floor plan v1 and its master wide v02 are superseded; v02's furniture and props carry over. The office views made from the approved private-office master are waiting for review (see Decisions).

## Concept

A cheap storefront unit in the **middle** of a single-story Albuquerque strip mall, in **2030**, between a vape shop and a tax preparer. It's a deliberate echo of grandpa's strip-mall practice. Sophie has made it look **smarter than it can afford**: wall-to-wall philosophy books, a slate chalkboard, one good leather chair for clients. The street side is gaudy Goodman yellow and red. The inside is warm, scholarly and a little shabby.

It is laid out like a real **solo practitioner's office**: clients walk into a small waiting room, and Sophie sees them in her own private office behind a solid wall and a door that closes. Confidentiality is part of the job, and the set should show it.

The 2030 touches stay small: a thin wireless display, a phone used as a document scanner, an AI-assistant speaker she never trusts. No holograms, no glass-cube futurism.

## How a real solo practice in a strip mall is laid out

This is what floor plan v2 copies from real small-practice offices in strip-mall units:

- **Mid-row unit, glass on one side only.** Units between two neighbors have party walls left and right, so all daylight comes from the storefront glass at the front and, at most, a small rear window by the steel back door.
- **Waiting room at the front.** A few mismatched chairs, a side table with magazines, a water cooler or coffee maker, a coat stand by the door, a small reception desk that is often empty in a one-person practice (with a desk bell), and one "important" piece on the wall to reassure clients.
- **A solid partition with a door** between the waiting room and the lawyer's office, so a client in the waiting room can't see or hear the consultation. Many solo practitioners put a white-noise machine outside the office door for the same reason.
- **The private office at the back.** Desk facing the door, the lawyer's back to the wall or window, two client chairs in front of the desk, bookshelves, filing cabinets, and diplomas on the wall the client faces.
- **A short hallway** from the waiting room past the office door to a restroom and the back door onto the service alley.

- **Waiting room** (front, full width): the storefront window with half-open venetian blinds on the left, the glass front door on the right. Waiting chairs under the window, the reception desk against the partition beside the hallway opening, facing the front door, the gaudy gold-framed painting on the partition wall facing the door, so it's the first thing a client sees.
- **Hallway** (right side, from the waiting room to the back): the office door on its left near the front, the white-noise machine on the floor by that door, the restroom and the steel back door at the end.
- **Sophie's office** (behind the partition, left two-thirds of the unit): enter from the hallway through a solid wood door. Desk in the middle, facing the door and the front of the building. Sophie's back is to the rear window and the bookcases. Two client chairs face the desk with their backs to the partition. The chalkboard and the diplomas are on the left wall (the party wall with the vape shop). Filing cabinets stand against the partition.

**Screen direction (unchanged rule):** when Sophie sits at her desk, the chalkboard is on her **right**. Seen from the client chairs, the chalkboard is on the **left** of frame and the office door is on the right, behind the client.

**Sun:** the storefront faces west, so late-afternoon golden light floods the waiting room through the front blinds. Sophie's rear window faces east, so her office gets low golden sun in the **morning**. At dusk and at night her office is lit by the banker's lamp.
