## Checks

- **The long speech:** compare **Extend** (which needs Omni Flash 1.2) with two matched generations joined by a cutaway. Record which one gives continuous lip sync and an unchanged face.
- **Group consistency:** the panel's appearance and seating order stay the same between sh01, sh02, sh05 and sh06.
- **Eyelines:** Sophie looks up toward the panel, and the panel looks down toward her.
- **The opponent's argument is credible:** the chair's "broken promise" point is a genuine deontological objection, and Sophie's reply challenges whether there was an obligation, not whether promises matter.
- The extras don't morph or duplicate.

**Stretch test:** a two-shot of Sophie and the Client where only Sophie speaks, to test whether Flow can keep the non-speaking character silent.
