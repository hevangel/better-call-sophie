## Description

```text
Has the internet decided you're a bad person? Dr. Sophie Goodman, philosophical advocate, won't say you're right. She'll make sure you get a good argument.

A 30-second in-world TV commercial for Goodman Philosophical Consulting, and the first technical demo for Better Call Sophie, an AI-generated philosophical drama in development.

HOW IT WAS MADE
AI-generated, with human direction. The showrunner set the direction, chose every take and approved the final cut.
• Pictures and dialogue: Google Flow (Omni 1.2 Flash), using one saved character for Sophie's face and voice
• Jingle and sung tag: Google Flow Music
• End-card lettering, edit and mix: ordinary editing tools (Python, ffmpeg)

UNOFFICIAL FAN WORK
Better Call Sophie is an unofficial, non-commercial fan concept inspired by the Breaking Bad / Better Call Saul universe. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators or rights holders of those series. All characters are fictional, and no real person's likeness or voice is used. The phone number is a fictional 555 number.

Dr. Goodman is not a lawyer. Nothing in this video is legal advice. Or moral advice. Probably.
```
