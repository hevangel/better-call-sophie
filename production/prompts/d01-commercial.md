# D01 commercial — shot prompts

**Status:** PROPOSED prompts, first used on 2026-11-23 for the D01 test run. Script and shots: [production/demo/d01-commercial-30s.md](../demo/d01-commercial-30s.md).

All shots: Google Flow video mode, **Ingredients**, 16:9, 720p, x1, model **Omni 1.2 Flash** (the composer shows "Video · 720p · 8s"; Flow makes 8 s clips, trimmed in the edit), Flow agent off, in the show project `Better Call Sophie - Flow Experiments` (`c9df3f16-dbf1-459b-a447-b20fbd978552`), where Sophie's character and the office views live.

**Mentions.** `@[Name]` marks an asset inserted with Flow's `@` picker (type `@`, search the name, select it, **Add to prompt**). `@[Sophie Goodman]` is her Flow character (approved face, body and voice Kore); `@[office-….jpg]` is an approved office view uploaded to the show project. Each mention also adds the asset as an ingredient. The jingle is added in post, so every prompt asks for no music.

## demo-d01-sh01 v01 (cam-c wide, turn from the board)

```text
@[Sophie Goodman] in her office, matching @[office-master-wide-golden-v03.jpg]: a static wide shot from the office door corner toward the chalkboard, the walnut desk and the bookcases, warm golden-hour light through half-open blinds, the green banker's lamp on. Sophie, in her deep burgundy pantsuit and cream blouse, small gold hoop earrings, dark wavy hair loosely pinned up, stands at the chalkboard holding a piece of chalk. She turns from the board to face the camera and says clearly in English, with the confident, slightly cheesy energy of a local TV commercial: "Has the internet decided you're a bad person?" Exactly one speaker and no one else in the room. Static camera. No music. No subtitles, no on-screen text, no logos, and no writing on the chalkboard beyond the trolley drawing.
```
